Power of Dua

Dua is the beautiful connection between a believer and Allah. Turn to Allah with hope, patience and trust. Your Dua is never meaningless.

✦ Understanding Dua ✦

The Power of Dua

Dua is an act of worship and a means of seeking help, guidance, mercy and blessings from Allah. It is a direct conversation with your Creator, a moment of vulnerability and hope.

❤️

Hope

Never lose hope in the mercy of Allah. He hears every sincere call.

🕊️

Peace

Dua can bring comfort and peace to the heart, easing anxiety and worry.

🌙

Trust

Ask Allah and place your trust in Him. He knows what is best for you.

وَقَالَ رَبُّكُمُ ادْعُونِي أَسْتَجِبْ لَكُمْ

"And your Lord says: Call upon Me; I will respond to you."

— Surah Ghafir (40:60)

Benefits of Making Dua

1

Act of Worship

The Prophet ﷺ said: "Dua is worship itself." (Tirmidhi)

2

Brings Peace

Dua calms the heart and removes anxiety and grief.

3

Changes Destiny

Dua can change what has been decreed by Allah's permission.

4

Protection

Dua protects from harm, evil eye and difficulties.

🕌

Etiquette of Making Dua

1

Start with Praise of Allah

Begin your Dua by praising Allah and sending blessings upon the Prophet ﷺ.

2

Face the Qibla

It is recommended to face the direction of the Kaaba while making Dua.

3

Have Certainty

Believe that Allah will answer your Dua. Do not doubt His mercy.

4

Be Patient

Allah answers in His own time. Never give up or lose hope.

5

Ask with Humility

Approach Allah with humility, recognizing your need for Him.

6

End with Durood

Conclude your Dua by sending blessings upon the Prophet ﷺ.

⏰

Best Times for Dua

🌙

Last Third of Night

When Allah descends to the lowest heaven and asks: "Who is calling upon Me?"

🕌

Between Adhan & Iqamah

A time when Dua is not rejected.

🌅

Friday Afternoon

The last hour before Maghrib on Friday.

💧

While Fasting

The Dua of a fasting person is not rejected.

✨

Never Stop Making Dua

Ask Allah with sincerity, patience and faith. Your Dua is never meaningless. Even if the answer is delayed, it is never denied.

مَنْ لَا يَسْأَلُ اللَّهَ يَغْضَبْ عَلَيْهِ

"Whoever does not ask Allah, He becomes angry with him."

— Sunan Ibn Majah
